[h2][b]Statera - A Balance, Immersion and Strategy Overhaul[/b][/h2] 

This overhaul focuses on 4 things:
[olist]
[*] [b]Fixing known issues of the game[/b] like lowering prices for settlement expand slots, increasing birth rates, etc.
[*] [b]Improving the balance[/b] of different aspects of the game like making all 5 knight types worth using.
[*] [b]Adding additional content[/b] like buildings or units to increase historical accuracy or strategic depth
[*] [b]Increasing thematic aspects[/b] trough f.e. adjusting unit names, descriptions etc. for certain kingdoms/cultures.
[/olist]

All of this is done to improve balance, strategic depth and replayability.
There are no radical solutions in this mod that just elimiate aspects of the game, my focus is make them fit better.
